# Optional Future Physical Validation

Status: **retained protocol; not a first-paper requirement**.

Decision date: 2026-08-28.

## Scope change

The first Causal4D paper is now limited to controlled and public-data evidence.
The previously registered same-object physical protocol---18 grasp sessions,
36 command executions, and independent-execution calibration---is no longer a
submission gate or the project's decisive next milestone.

The protocol remains scientifically valid as an optional future extension. Its
current evidence state is:

```text
specified sessions:   18
specified executions: 36
acquired executions:   0
paper requirement:     false
```

No positive or negative physical result is inferred from `0/36`. The public-data
paper must neither imply that the protocol was executed nor describe its absence
as an incomplete public-data result.

## Why the protocol is retained

The registered design remains useful if a future collaborator has suitable
hardware and elects to test:

- factual continuation on repeated physical executions;
- chronological same-grasp action transfer;
- fresh-contact transfer;
- execution-level calibration; and
- independently measured actuation, contact, slip, reset, and registration.

Those questions cannot be answered fully by public records. They are therefore
future validation opportunities rather than prerequisites for the bounded first
paper.

## Nonblocking relationship to the public-data paper

The active paper evidence is:

1. controlled latent-contact abduction;
2. topology-aware exact-identity versus predictive-equivalence diagnostics;
3. the target-closed Deform360 held-out-action result;
4. the retained PokeFlex source-backend negative; and
5. the released single-interaction uncertainty and discrepancy audit.

These studies support the exact claims in
[causal4d_paper_scope.md](causal4d_paper_scope.md). The hardware protocol may not
block, rescue, or retroactively redefine those claims.

## Preservation rules

The historical protocol, amendments, schedules, readiness tooling, and evidence
records remain immutable provenance. In particular:

- do not relabel scaffolds, dry runs, source panels, or workflow artifacts as
  confirmatory physical executions;
- do not change the recorded `0/36` count without genuine physical acquisition;
- do not claim independent pre-acquisition attestation under the v5
  single-operator amendment;
- do not use public-data outcomes to rewrite a future hardware target schedule;
  and
- do not interpret closure of the active acquisition issues as a scientific
  negative result.

## Future execution, if independently chosen

A future execution must issue a new current operational plan that explicitly
re-adopts the frozen design, binds the then-current software/environment, and
passes all safety, object-registration, synchronization, support, dry-run, and
target-access gates before execution 1. The detailed historical runbooks remain
available in this repository and in Git history.

Until such a plan is deliberately reactivated, no acquisition automation or
hardware command is authorized by this document.
